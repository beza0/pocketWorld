**App name (30):** Pocket World: A Country a Day
**Subtitle / short description (30 / 80):** Learn geography in a minute · A new country every day: flag, map, a quick fact and games.

**Description**

Which country will you learn today?

Pocket World brings you a new country every day. A real photo, the flag, its place on the map, the capital, languages and one memorable fact — in about a minute. If you want more, continue with its culture, food and places to see.

• Country of the Day: every one of 195 countries once in each 195-day cycle.
• Today's 3 Questions: lock it in with the flag, the map and the fact.
• Daily Mystery: three clues, four options. The earlier you know, the more points.
• My Discovery Map: colour in the countries you discovered, continent by continent.
• Free Play: Flags, Map, Short Facts, Photos and Mixed; Easy and Hard.
• All 195 countries: 193 UN members, Palestine and the Vatican.
• Works offline. No ads, no sign-up required.
• Turkish and English; country names always shown in both.
• Optional account to back up your progress across devices.

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons; each photo's author and licence is shown in the app.
